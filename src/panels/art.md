## Introductions

I am an enthusiast photographer who mostly enjoys landscape and travel photography on both analog and digital systems! You can see a more detailed collection of my works in my [art blog]({{ "/art-blog/" | url }}). Additionally, I post some of the pieces on my instagram ([@jwu_arts](https://www.instagram.com/jwu_arts/)) account, so feel free to give it a follow if you are interested in seeing more of my works.

Besides photography, I also enjoy making digital pieces in my free time. Pixel art, with its nostalgic charm, challenges me to express detailed ideas within the constraints of limited resolution and color-palletes, while vector art allows me to fuse my appreciation for technology into artistic expressions.
