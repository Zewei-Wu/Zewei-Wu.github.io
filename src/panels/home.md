## Introduction

Welcome to my personal website!

I am a PhD student in Astrophysics at the [University of California San Diego](https://astro.ucsd.edu/) and an [LSST Data Science Fellow](https://lsstdiscoveryalliance.org/lsst-discovery-alliance-programs/data-science-fellowship/data-science-fellows/). I’m interested in pursuing projects applying computational methods onto fundamental Astrophysics. My current work focuses on galaxy and dark matter simulations.

Outside of research, I am determined to uplift emerging scientists and lower the barrier to theoretical sciences.

Feel free to look around, explore my [research]({{ "/research/" | url }}) and [art]({{ "/art/" | url }}) sections, or access my [contact information and CV]({{ "/cv/" | url }}) here.

## Background

I am natively from Beijing, China. I was selected into the Zaopei program of the [RDFZ](https://www.rdfz.cn/en/) middle school, where I developed an interest in science and specifically astronomy. I subsequently went to the United States for high school, and spent my formative years in Pittsburgh. After graduating from [Shady Side Academy](https://www.shadysideacademy.org/schools/senior-school) with a Summa Cum Laude, I attended the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/en) with a B.S. in Astrophysics and graduating with honors. I now pursue a Ph.D. in Astrophysics at the [University of California San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/) with PI Ethan Nadler as an [LSST Data Science Fellow](https://lsstdiscoveryalliance.org/lsst-discovery-alliance-programs/data-science-fellowship/data-science-fellows/).
