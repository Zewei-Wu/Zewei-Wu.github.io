- As a part of my curriculum at UChicago, I had the opportunity to visit Chile for an observational run. The Magellan Telescopes, perched high above the world in the arid Chilean desert, offer some of the best optical visibility conditions on the planet. The high altitude and the extreme dry environment work together to provide a clear window into our cosmos.
- This single, 250-second exposure encapsulates the intersection of human curiosity and the wonders of the universe. It serves as a poignant reminder of our place in the cosmos, and our ongoing quest in the field of astrophysics.
