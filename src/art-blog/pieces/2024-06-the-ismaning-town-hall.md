- I briefly stayed near Garching bei München where I interned at the Max Planck Institute for summer of 2024. The town's relaxed atmosphere made it perfect for me to wind down after an intense academic year.
- This photo reflects this moment of tranquility: I had just arrived at a foreign country and taken the train to an unfamiliar town. The solemn ringings of the bell tower halted me on my journey to the hotel. Here, I reclaimed a slowness to life which was lost to me for a long time.
