- Biking around the ring was one of my most fond memories at Fermilab. That day was going to be no different, but my passions were curbed by a sudden thunderstorm, which forced me into hiding.
- I had just came out from a window sill, where I took shelter through the thunderstorm – the sun had done the same, it seemed, generously casting its rays onto the bike and the grass nearby.
