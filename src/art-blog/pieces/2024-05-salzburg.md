- With my friend visiting me in Munich, we took a trip to Salzburg, Austria. The city can be descrabed by a brush of green and blue, decorated with the traditional architectures of churches and castles.
- Ascending to the top of the mountains, we are able to see down onto the vast flatlands, rolling into the Austrian alps down South. It was a view I won't ever forget.
