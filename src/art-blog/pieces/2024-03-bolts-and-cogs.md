- As a part of my curriculum at UChicago, I had the opportunity to visit Chile for an observational run. The pile of scrap metals and parts you see are remnants of our ongoing efforts to study the cosmos.
- This image is a tribute to the unseen aspects of our journey in astrophysics, a reminder that even in the face of the infinite cosmos, every bolt, gear, and scrap of metal finds its tangible place.
- [Featured](https://www.flickr.com/photos/195258204@N03/53628409279/in/album-72177720315909277) in the 2024 UChicago Science As Art competition.
