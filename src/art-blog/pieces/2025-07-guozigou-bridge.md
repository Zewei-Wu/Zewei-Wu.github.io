- Against a backdrop of rugged green mountains and snow-capped peaks, cables rise like a modern monument in an ancient landscape.
- The Guozigou Bridge is a marvel of engineering on the Lianhuo Expressway, which runs across the entirety of China. Countless shipping trucks soar through this bridge every day carrying goods from Kazakhstan to the Pacific.
