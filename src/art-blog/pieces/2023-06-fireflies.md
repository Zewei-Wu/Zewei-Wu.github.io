- I saw fireflies for the first time at Fermilab, where it was dark and unpolluted enough for them to exist naturally. Those fleeting green dots captured my interests for quite a few days.
- This photo was ontained from stacking together multiple 10.0s exposure shots. This was done to show the fireflies more clearly while not overblowing the background with a single exposure.
